## Primary legal checks

- Victoria's mental-injury eligibility rules changed for injuries on or after
  31 March 2024 and require a diagnosed mental injury, significant dysfunction,
  predominant employment causation, and exclusions including usual or typical
  stress/burnout: [WorkSafe Victoria](https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/practice-directive-mental-injury-eligibility).
- Commonwealth and State environmental approval tracks remain distinct, and a
  controlled action needs formal assessment and approval before it starts:
  [DCCEEW](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/environment/epbc/approvals).
- The federal NDB scheme requires reasonable and expeditious assessment, with
  reasonable steps to finish within 30 days, and a statement to the Commissioner
  as soon as practicable once an eligible breach is established:
  [Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) ss 26WH and 26WK](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712/latest/text).
- A WA de facto property or financial application generally must be filed within
  two years after breakdown: [Family Court of Western Australia](https://familycourt.wa.gov.au/P/property_and_financial.aspx).
- Current ART guidance confirms that visa-review availability and strict,
  non-extendable time limits depend on the decision and notice:
  [Administrative Review Tribunal](https://www.art.gov.au/applying-review/immigration-and-citizenship).
- The ordinary Queensland progress-payment pathway contains payment claims,
  payment schedules, consequences of non-response/non-payment, and adjudication:
  [Building Industry Fairness (Security of Payment) Act 2017 (Qld), ch 3](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/pdf/current/act-2017-043).
- The High Court continues to require disciplined treatment of interstate
  intermediate appellate authority: [Hill v Zuda Pty Ltd](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/judgments/judgments-1998-current/hill-v-zuda-pty-ltd) and
  [Lendlease Corporation Ltd v Pallas](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/judgments/judgments-1998-current/lendlease-corporation-limited-v-pallas).
